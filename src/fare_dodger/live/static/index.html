How to play
  • Two teams play rounds of about 4 minutes, each on a different district: one team runs, the other hunts (with two extra bots), and a random team runs first. Empty places are played by bots.
  • Every Dodger is a point: escaping scores for the runners, getting caught for the hunters (a 2v2 round can end 1–1). First past half the points wins; sides switch at halftime, and after a tie every round until one team leads by two.
  • Dodger: survive the round. Jog the streets (freely across parks), ride real trams, buses and metros, and stay out of the Bounty Hunters' reach. Buy a ticket (T) or risk the inspectors; grab a coffee (C) at a ☕ to run fast.
  • Bounty Hunter: catch the Dodger on foot or on the same vehicle. You only see them up close; follow the reports on the minimap and the arrow at the screen edge.
  • WASD move (up is the top of your screen) · right-drag turn the map · N north up · E or the switch (bottom right) get on / get off / stay on · 1–6 pick a departure · +/− zoom · Esc back here.
