# 0016. The jail: a race after an unpaid fine

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-29

## Context
After ADR 0005's 2026-10-29 addendum, a Dodger who could not pay a fine was held where they
got off for three timetable minutes (30 real seconds). The owner found that excessive and
wanted something more fun. Their idea: the Dodger is taken to a jail and held there for about
30 seconds, so there is a chance to escape and a chance to get caught. The jail should look
good.

## Decision
- **The jail is a real police station.**
  - `amenity=police` comes with each district's landmark download (`LANDMARKS_VERSION` 2).
  - The jail is the police station nearest the middle of the network, among those within
    400 m of a station, so the Bounty Hunters can get there by transport (`pick_jail`).
  - Police stations are never package destinations.
  - Without police data, the jail is a police post at the station nearest the middle
    (`World.jail_spot`).
  - On real data, Inner Pest gets the VIII. kerületi Rendőrkapitányság, 230 m from Rákóczi
    tér.
- **The rule** (`LiveGame._jail`):
  - A Dodger who cannot pay the whole fine is taken straight from the stop to the jail.
  - They are held there for `jail_s` (30) *real* seconds: the time scale is applied, so the
    setting matches what the player sees.
  - The Bounty Hunters are told where the Dodger is. While held, the Dodger is on foot, so
    a Bounty Hunter within `catch_radius_m` catches them.
  - When the door opens, they have paid all they had and are free. A fine that can be paid
    is unchanged: a short hold at the stop.
- **Bots:**
  - A report now says how long the Dodger is known to stay put (`Ping.until`).
  - The bot Bounty Hunters spread over a smaller area (the Dodger can't move yet).
  - A bot that reaches the reported station while the Dodger is still held walks on to the
    exact spot. Bot journeys end at stations, and a jail can be a street away. Afterwards it
    walks back to the network.
- **The look:**
  - The 3D toy town draws a barred cell (`makeJail`) sized for one person and scaled with
    the people. It has:
    - a concrete base and steel bars;
    - a padlocked yellow door and a bench;
    - a police-blue roof with yellow "BÖRTÖN" signs front and back;
    - a red and blue siren that flashes while someone is locked in.
  - The cell is tall and its roof does not overhang, so from the game's camera (about 40°)
    the prisoner shows between the front bars.
  - It is drawn after the people's see-through silhouettes, so the prisoner never shows as
    a ghost under the roof.
  - The flat map has a blue "🚔 BÖRTÖN" badge that flashes red and blue while the jail is
    busy, and the minimap has a dot.
  - The snapshot carries `jail` (name, position, busy) and `jailed`.
  - The model sheet (`/models.html`) shows the cell with a prisoner.

## Consequences
- **Balance:** in 200 bot rounds on Inner Pest against three bot Bounty Hunters, the bot
  Dodger escapes 57% of rounds. It was 62% with the three-minute hold, and 52% when not
  paying lost the round. Catches on foot are 36%: the race to the jail is real.
- **Camping:** a Bounty Hunter who waits at the jail door wins any race to it, but only
  when someone is fined and cannot pay. Since the reports are public, that is a choice
  between camping and hunting.
- **Districts without police data** use the fallback post until their landmarks download.
